# Contributors

Recognition here is **social only**. Per ADR-0024, capability is never a reward: listing in
this file and in release notes is the whole of it. There are no perks, no unlocked features,
no tiers, and no badges that gate anything, and declining or never being listed changes nothing
about what the software does.

## How listing works

Listing is **opt-in**: add yourself in a pull request (handle or name, plus one line on what
you did) or ask a maintainer to add you. Removal is unconditional — ask and you come off,
no questions and no record-keeping grudge.

Two contribution classes are credited **equally**:

- **Code, checks, design and research** — the usual kind.
- **Outcome reports** — answering the task-close questions (goal achieved? what was missing?
  a better approach in hindsight?). This is a real contribution, not a courtesy: those answers
  are the human-verdict labels the β instrument is short of, and ADR-0002 shows those labels
  are the scarcest input in the system.
