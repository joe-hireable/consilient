Thank you for Your interest in the Consilience project (the "**Project**") maintained by
Joseph Brown of Hertford, England (the "**Maintainer**").

This Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement ("**Agreement**") sets out the terms on which
the corporation, partnership or other entity identified below ("**You**") grants rights in
Contributions made by its employees and contractors. It does not transfer ownership: You
retain all right, title and interest in Your Contributions.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**You**" means the entity identified in the schedule below, together with any other
entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that entity.
"Control" means (i) the power, direct or indirect, to direct or manage such entity, whether
by contract or otherwise, (ii) ownership of fifty per cent (50%) or more of the outstanding
shares, or (iii) beneficial ownership of such entity.

1.2 "**Contribution**", "**Submitted**" and "**Original work of authorship**" have the
meanings given in the Project's Individual Contributor Licence Agreement, which applies
mutatis mutandis.

1.3 "**Designated Employees**" means the individuals listed in the schedule below, as
updated from time to time in accordance with clause 6.

## 2. Copyright licence

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, You grant to the Maintainer and to recipients of
software distributed by the Maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge,
royalty-free, irrevocable licence in and to Your Contributions to reproduce, prepare
derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, communicate to the public,
sublicense and distribute Your Contributions and such derivative works, **under any licence
terms, including licence terms that differ from those under which the Project is generally
made available**.

This licence is subject to the commitments in the Project's Relicensing Promise
(`docs/legal/RELICENSING-PROMISE.md` as at the date of this Agreement), which is
incorporated by reference.

## 3. Patent licence

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, You grant to the Maintainer and to recipients of
software distributed by the Maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge,
royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated below) patent licence to make, have made, use,
offer to sell, sell, import and otherwise transfer Your Contributions, where such licence
applies only to those patent claims licensable by You that are necessarily infringed by Your
Contributions alone or by combination of Your Contributions with the Project to which such
Contributions were Submitted.

If any entity institutes patent litigation against You or any other entity (including a
cross-claim or counterclaim in a lawsuit) alleging that a Contribution, or the Project to
which a Contribution has been made, constitutes direct or contributory patent infringement,
then any patent licences granted to that entity under this Agreement for that Contribution
or Project shall terminate as of the date such litigation is filed.

## 4. Moral rights

To the fullest extent permitted by law, You procure the unconditional and irrevocable waiver
of, and undertake not to assert, any moral rights and rights of a similar nature in the
Contributions, including without limitation the rights conferred by sections 77 to 85 of the
Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988. Where such rights cannot be waived, You procure
that they are not enforced against the Maintainer, its licensees, sublicensees or
successors.

## 5. Your warranties

You represent and warrant that:

5.1 You are legally entitled to grant the licences in clauses 2 and 3;

5.2 each Designated Employee is authorised to Submit Contributions on Your behalf;

5.3 each Contribution is an original creation of a Designated Employee, save as disclosed
under clause 7;

5.4 to the best of Your knowledge, the Contributions do not infringe the intellectual
property rights of any third party; and

5.5 You will notify the Maintainer promptly if You become aware of any facts or
circumstances that would make any of these warranties inaccurate.

## 6. Designated Employees

You shall maintain the list of Designated Employees in the schedule below, notifying the
Maintainer in writing of any additions or removals. Contributions Submitted by an individual
before their removal from the list remain licensed under this Agreement. Contributions
Submitted by an individual who is not a Designated Employee are governed by the Individual
Contributor Licence Agreement, if accepted by that individual.

## 7. Third-party materials

Work that is not an original creation of a Designated Employee may be Submitted separately,
identifying its source and any licence or other restriction of which You are aware, and
conspicuously marked "Submitted on behalf of a third party: [named third party]".

## 8. No warranty, no support

Except for the warranties in clause 5, and to the fullest extent permitted by law, You
provide the Contributions "as is", without warranties or conditions of any kind, express or
implied. You are not expected to provide support for the Contributions.
