# The Relicensing Promise

**Version 1.0 — DRAFT, pending legal review.**

For the Consilience project (`joe-hireable/consilient`).

This document is incorporated by reference into the Individual and Corporate Contributor
Licence Agreements. It is a **binding commitment**, not a statement of intent.

---

## Why this exists

The Contributor Licence Agreements ask you for something a bare MIT contribution does not:
the right to distribute your work under different licence terms, including commercial ones.
That is a real ask, and contributors are right to be wary of it. The history of open source
contains enough licence rug-pulls that scepticism is the correct default.

This document is the other half of the bargain. It states what the Maintainer will never do.

## The commitments

**1. Released code stays MIT, permanently.**
Any version of the Project that has been publicly released under the MIT Licence remains
available under the MIT Licence in perpetuity. The Maintainer will not withdraw, revoke or
attempt to revoke that licence for any released version, and will not remove released
versions from public distribution channels under the Maintainer's control.

**2. Relicensing applies only to derivative or downstream works.**
The right granted in clause 2 of the Contributor Licence Agreements will be exercised only
to distribute Contributions as part of a *separate* or *derivative* work under different
terms. It will not be used to close the Project itself.

**3. No retrospective closure.**
If the Maintainer ever ceases to release the Project under the MIT Licence, the last
MIT-licensed release remains MIT-licensed and will not be taken down. Contributors and users
may fork it.

**4. Attribution is preserved.**
Contributors' authorship records — commit history, `AUTHORS`, and any equivalent — will be
preserved in any relicensed or derivative distribution, notwithstanding the moral rights
waiver in the Contributor Licence Agreements.

**5. Advance notice.**
The Maintainer will give at least **ninety (90) days'** public notice, in the Project's
repository and on any Project mailing list or announcement channel, before first
distributing the Project or a substantial derivative of it under non-open-source terms.

**6. This promise cannot be weakened retrospectively.**
The Maintainer may publish amended versions of this document. An amendment applies only to
Contributions Submitted after it is published. Every Contribution remains governed by the
version of this promise in force when it was Submitted, and the Maintainer will retain and
publish every version.

**7. If the Project is transferred, the promise transfers with it.**
Any assignment or transfer of the Project, or of the benefit of the Contributor Licence
Agreements, is conditional on the transferee agreeing in writing to be bound by this promise
in respect of Contributions Submitted before the transfer.

## What this promise does not do

It does not stop the Maintainer building commercial products, hosted services or
proprietary derivatives that incorporate Contributions. That is precisely the right the
Contributor Licence Agreements grant, and this promise does not take it back. It constrains
*how* that right is exercised, not *whether*.

If you are not comfortable with that, please do not sign the Contributor Licence Agreement.
Forking the MIT-licensed Project and contributing to your fork is a legitimate choice and
the Maintainer will not treat it as hostile.

## Status

Commitments 1–7 are contractual terms, incorporated into each Contributor Licence Agreement
by reference and enforceable by the contributor who accepted that Agreement.
