# How this is funded

- **Document class:** W
- **Review by:** 2026-09-24
- **Falsifier:** ADR-0024 §1 or ADR-0048 §1 is superseded, or a capability exists that only works by contacting a server we operate. Either kills the claim that every capability is in the free version.

Class-W admission only. [asserted]

---

MIT licensed, open-source first. Every capability is in the free version, permanently, usable by someone who pays nothing and never contacts a server we operate. A feature that only works hosted is a feature built wrong.

**Open-core is refused.** Not one feature is held back to drive a commercial adoption. Capability is never a reward, a tier, or a licence key.

## What that means in practice

- You can run this on your own machine, with your own models or your own keys, and get the whole product.
- Local training of an open-weight model on your own data, on your own device, pays nothing and tells us nothing.
- Nothing is transmitted by default. Consent, when it exists, is per purpose, previewable on your own data, and revocable. Training a commercial product on your data is never bundled with "help us improve".

## What paid plans are, when they exist

Documented, not built. No paid code exists, and none will before the open-source release.

When they exist, they fund maintenance, hosted record storage, and hosted inference — facilitation of something you could run yourself but do not want to — at minimal margin, **prepaid, never in arrears**. Never for the capability itself, never for a limit lifted.

Prepaid because billing in arrears requires attribution to work perfectly, and this project has already measured its metered composition failing. A prepaid job cannot start until the ceiling is covered. Comparables and the arithmetic live in the design record, not here; a price copied into this page would be a second source of truth.

Far-future ideas about piping someone else's cloud bill are queued well beyond launch. They have a precondition that, if met, dissolves most of the rationale. Design nothing for them now.

## How it is funded today

By **sponsorship**, not by selling the software. That route sells nothing, takes no commission, and does not turn the maintainer into a trader in respect of the product. No affiliate revenue, no consolidated billing, no hosted paid tier and no advertising is implemented.

If that ever changes, it will be a new decision, written as one, not an adjustment to this page.
