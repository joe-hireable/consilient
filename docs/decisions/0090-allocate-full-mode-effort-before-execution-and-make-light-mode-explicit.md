# 0090. Allocate full-mode effort before execution and make light mode explicit

- **Status:** PROVISIONAL — EXP-133 tests whether any numeric phase default is earned; EXP-134 can
  remove frontier-supervised weaker execution
- **Date:** 2026-08-23
- **Deciders:** Codex dispatch `20260823T095314-20a91056d9` for the provisional mechanism. Joe
  Brown's verbatim 23 August product direction is input, not authorship of this decision.
- **Inquiry tier reached:** T1 ground; T3 pre-registered as EXP-133 and EXP-134, not run
- **Executable model:** none — phase quality and supervision cost require matched real artefacts and
  blinded outcomes; an assumed model would manufacture the unknowns

## Context

The principal first offered 80/20 as an example, corrected it from a target to a hypothesis, then
superseded the ratio entirely: locate the bar, exceed it through executable reasoning, and realise the
candidate; transition when the better-than-best threshold passes, not when a percentage elapses. He
also specified frontier instruction/review of weaker execution, no parallelism for its own sake, a
better-than-best default and a quick single-model chat that bypasses the scientific machinery.
[measured: `../00-context/the-machine-2026-08-22.md`, "The phase transition is a threshold, not a
ratio"]

No current source records this phase taxonomy or threshold. `scripts/dispatch.py` caps individual
children, `budget.py` admits OpenRouter spend, and `events.py` records no request-wide phase plan,
category charge or transition receipt. [measured]

The incoming brief also says beta sizes squads and gives the iid logarithmic ceiling as policy. That
is wrong under ADR-0077: beta bounds shippable candidate exposure through the dependence-robust
`floor(epsilon / q_upper)` policy, while ADR-0067 separately governs evidence-role count through one
Owner and distinct decision-relevant anchors. [measured] [algebra]

ADR-0002 already owns cheap-first cascading and capability-gap admission. ADR-0030 owns declared
context before selection. ADR-0067 owns one-Owner composition, ADR-0077 owns candidate exposure and
fusion, ADR-0081 owns the high-consequence consilience gate, ADR-0087 owns direct answers, and the
Inquiry tier owns how much decision rigour is warranted. A new decision must join those contracts
rather than rebuild them. [measured]

The full operational contract is
[`2026-08-23-effort-allocation.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-08-23-effort-allocation.md).
[measured]

## Decision

Consilient will expose `light` and `full` scheduling modes and separate `fast` and
`better-than-best` full-mode profiles; better-than-best is the default for durable, material and
principal-used work, while fast must pass the linked specification's reversible, low-consequence,
bounded, objective and no-missing-fact gates for an already sealed decision package. Every full
application unit will carry one category from which decide/do accounting is derived. Better-than-best
will additionally label units `locate`, `exceed` or `realise`, enforce one total budget plus a task-
specific `exceed` give-up ceiling and an atomically protected realisation reserve, move to realisation only on an ADR-0081-backed `bar_beaten` receipt
or an honest `bar_not_beaten` give-up, and report rather than configure the observed phase ratio.
Fast will instead require a `fast_admitted` receipt and realise only the sealed package under its
ordinary verifier, with no better-than-best claim.
Objectively assessable execution may use one frontier Owner to seal an instruction, supervise a
weaker executor through two bounded retries and one frontier correction, quarantine every draft and
run held-out acceptance once; structural, authority, verifier or total-budget failure terminates the
loop, and three reviews may cost more than paired direct generation. Light will use one model and
supplied context while explicitly giving up beta accounting, the consilience gate, squads, discovery,
research, simulation, experimentation, the decision protocol and independent verification. No mode,
role or model receives evidence weight, and none may delegate the principal's verdict, approval,
spend, credential, publication or gate authority. [measured: ADR-0081 authority boundary] [asserted]

## Evidence

- `[measured]` The principal's dated words supersede the ratio with a better-than-best phase
  transition and a required category taxonomy; they also specify the supervision shape, no idle
  compute, better-than-best default and quick single-model facility.
- `[measured]` Current dispatch and budget paths have child-level caps and spend admission but no
  request-wide phase/category ledger, bar contract or transition receipt.
- `[measured]` Superpowers v6.3.0 is installed at commit `b36e0829…`; its pinned brainstorming and
  writing-plans skills supply scalable design and exact TDD planning but no retrieval, simulation,
  experiment, phase-budget or beta contract.
- `[cited]` Superpowers v6.3.0, PR #2063 and its evaluation artefacts are recorded at bibliography
  section 19. They establish a serious workflow comparator, not application-outcome superiority.
- `[cited]` Yang et al. (arXiv:2608.14927v1) show on paired reasoning benchmarks that predicting
  failure is easier than predicting which collaboration protocol pays; Schmalbach
  (arXiv:2606.17099v1) finds explicit coding-delegation contracts improved reviewability but not
  saturated correctness while adding tokens and wall time. They make direct, cost and review-time
  comparisons mandatory, but neither establishes application-building superiority.
- `[measured]` ADR-0087 already answers directly when another observation would not change the
  decision; ADR-0067 already defaults to one Owner; ADR-0077 already separates candidate exposure
  from evidence roles; ADR-0081 refuses high-consequence single-anchor conclusions.
- `[asserted]` Recording dispatches and tokens by phase/category will make the ratio hypothesis
  testable without laundering it into policy. EXP-133 compares threshold-triggered allocation,
  80/20, 20/80 and pinned Superpowers.
- `[asserted]` Frontier instruction plus diagnostic correction will match direct-frontier quality at
  lower frontier usage often enough to justify the loop. EXP-134 is the killing check.

## Evidence against

- `[asserted]` The 80/20 may be backwards. Deciding may be cheap while integration, migration,
  browser behaviour, deployment, debugging and user contact are the expensive sources of truth.
  An execution-heavy competitor can ship, observe and iterate while this system spends four fifths
  of its inference budget deliberating over a world it has not touched.
- `[measured]` EXP-16's one-agent arm won 9 of 12 substituted model-family judgements; its Owner
  meeting won 2 of 12 at 4.8 times the tokens and 3.7 times the wall time. The registered human
  ground-truth judgement was never obtained, so this measures substituted preference and overhead,
  not human-labelled quality.
- `[cited]` Superpowers upstream removed an independent subagent review loop after a five-version by
  five-trial comparison added about 25 minutes without measurable plan-quality improvement. Review
  can be ceremony even when its intent is quality.
- `[asserted]` Three full-diff frontier reviews and correction packets can cost more frontier tokens
  than generating directly, while adding context loss and latency. The weak executor may be a false
  economy.
- `[asserted]` A reserved-token ratio is a weak proxy for compute: it omits input tokens, treats model
  tokenisers and capabilities alike, and does not measure intelligence, FLOPs or useful reasoning.
- `[asserted]` Discovery, research, modelling and experimentation can produce a polished paper trail
  without improving the chosen application. A fixed 80% floor can institutionalise analysis rather
  than discipline it.

The strongest objection may be right. This decision therefore does not describe 80/20 as best
practice or fact. EXP-133 includes a 20/80 inverse arm and the pinned Superpowers workflow at equal
total budget. A result may support a later numeric proposal; until then no ratio is configured.
EXP-134 separately removes supervision when quality or frontier-cost conditions fail. [asserted]

## Consequences

**Positive** — application work receives visible category accounting under one hard total;
better-than-best realisation cannot begin without an evidence-bearing threshold/give-up receipt;
fast and light become predictable
rather than informal shortcuts. [asserted]

**Negative** — full application work may deliver less code, later. Phase accounting adds admission
and event contracts, and supervision can duplicate frontier context work before falling back to the
frontier anyway. [asserted]

**Neutral but load-bearing** — no phase ratio is enforced; beta governs candidate exposure, distinct
anchors govern evidence roles, independent work units govern concurrency, and no mode changes
principal authority or gate state. [asserted]

## What would overturn this

EXP-133 can support a successor ADR proposing 80/20, 20/80 or continued threshold-triggered
allocation under its frozen paired outcome, correctness and cost rules. Today there is nothing
numeric to remove or invert. A result that is broader but not better is a loss of the better-than-
Superpowers claim. [asserted]

EXP-134 retains frontier-supervised weaker execution only if quality is non-inferior and both
frontier-equivalent cost and total provider-equivalent cost improve by the pre-registered margins.
It removes the route on the pre-registered quality, frontier-cost, total-cost, critical-error or
three-correction/economic-loss rule. The direct-frontier path remains. [asserted]

Light mode is a separately stated product requirement. It is overturned only by the principal or by
a measured safety failure specific to protocol-free chat; a failure of the phase mechanism does not
silently delete it. [asserted]

## Publication candidate?

**No.** The threshold is asserted, the numeric ratio is only a hypothesis, the compute proxy is
incomplete, and neither killing experiment has run. A publication candidate would require replicated
outcome advantage over threshold-triggered allocation, the inverse and the pinned external workflow,
with complete adverse and resource data. [asserted]
