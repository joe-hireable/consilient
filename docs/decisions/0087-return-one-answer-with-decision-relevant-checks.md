# 0087. Return one answer with decision-relevant checks and answer directly when convergence adds no value

- **Status:** PROPOSED — EXP-128 is deliberately unwritten; ADR-0077 is present, not accepted, and owns fusion/exposure
- **Date:** 2026-08-22
- **Deciders:** Codex dispatch `20260822T162045-1b18d15367` for the proposed mechanism; Joe Brown's
  quoted product direction in the dispatch brief is input, not authorship of this decision
- **Inquiry tier reached:** T1 ground; T3 is a verified-free but unregistered EXP-128 proposal
- **Executable model:** none — perceived preference, correctness and cost require a blind comparison,
  not assumed functional forms

## Context

All 17 pre-existing specifications named by the brief were read. None defines the final user-visible answer or a
blind perceived-quality comparison with a strong single-model response, although several already
define internal quality mechanisms. The narrow response-and-measurement gap is measured; a claim
that the repository had no answer-quality mechanism at all would be false. [measured]

The frozen whole-product bar is ChatGPT Work, while Hermes and Ruflo falsify broad novelty claims for
delegation, durable coordination, cross-harness memory and self-evaluation. A visible response cannot
win by displaying those mechanisms or by naming more models. [measured] [asserted]

ADR-0067 already owns one-Owner composition and ADR-0081 owns high-consequence structural-anchor
admission. ADR-0077 is tracked and PROVISIONAL and owns candidate exposure, composite verification
and evidence fusion. This ADR depends on that live contract and neither duplicates nor supersedes
it; implementation remains blocked while the fusion dependency is PROVISIONAL and EXP-128 remains
unwritten. [measured] [asserted]

## Decision

Consilient will return one primary answer from one accountable Owner. It will lead with the useful
outcome, show only source-bound claims, executed results and genuinely tried comparisons in a compact
`Checked` line, and name a concrete falsifier for a material recommendation. It will default to one
Owner and answer directly—saying so—when no unavailable decision-changing observation is worth its
cost. Agent count, votes, model confidence, raw transcripts, streaming tokens and visible reasoning
will never be presented as evidence. The full contract is
[`2026-08-22-answer-quality.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-08-22-answer-quality.md). [asserted]

This remains PROPOSED. `EXP-128` was verified free, but its entry is intentionally unwritten because
this dispatch may not edit the experiment register; the experiment is not pre-registered and no
visible-quality claim is authorised. [measured] [asserted]

## Evidence

- `[measured]` The 17 pre-existing specifications contain internal fusion, gating, capability, delivery and
  verification mechanisms but no final answer contract or perceived-quality comparison.
- `[measured]` The product bar names ChatGPT Work as the strongest general delegated-work product and
  finds no public product-level accepted-outcome or verifier false-accept result.
- `[measured]` Hermes supplies nested delegation and durable coordination; Ruflo supplies a real
  Claude/Codex path, shared persistent memory and a held-out evaluation flywheel. These are not
  available as novelty claims.
- `[measured]` EXP-16's single-agent arm won 9 of 12 substituted blind model-grader best calls; its
  Owner meeting won 2 of 12 at `4.8×` the tokens and `3.7×` the wall time. The registered human
  ground truth was not obtained.
- `[cited]` Fok and Weld (2024), *In Search of Verifiability*, arXiv:2305.07722, find that
  explanations rarely yield complementary performance unless they reduce verification cost.
- `[cited]` Ao, Gao and Simchi-Levi (2026), *On the Reliability Limits of LLM-Based Multi-Agent
  Planning*, arXiv:2603.26993, prove weak dominance by an ideal central decision-maker when
  delegation introduces no exogenous signal.
- `[cited]` Kim et al. (2026), *Capable language models can outgrow the benefits of collaboration*,
  doi:10.1038/s42256-026-01268-y, find task-dependent gains and losses and multi-agent degradation on
  SWE-bench Verified across their matched configurations.
- `[cited]` Zhou (2026), *More Convincing, Not More Correct*, arXiv:2607.05904v1, reports judge
  acceptance rising to `0.938 ± 0.016` while hidden normalised exact-match accuracy was
  `0.202 ± 0.005` in the stated condition.
- `[asserted]` Claim-bound source and execution receipts will make a useful difference visible without
  forcing a user to inspect the organisation that produced it.

## Evidence against

- `[asserted]` Every visible feature in this decision can be produced by one well-tooled Owner; no
  user-facing element is structurally exclusive to a multi-agent system.
- `[measured]` The available substituted blind model-grader result favours the single-agent control;
  its registered human ground truth remains absent, and no perceived-quality comparison has run.
- `[cited]` Ao, Gao and Simchi-Levi imply that delegation with the same facts cannot improve the ideal
  central decision. More models over shared evidence are echo, not an answer to the theorem.
- `[cited]` Kim et al. measured multi-agent degradation on SWE-bench Verified; Jwalapuram et al.
  (2026), arXiv:2606.13003, found generic multi-agent systems losing to a strong single-agent
  comparator while their positive specialist result was deliberately separable and synthetic.
- `[cited]` Kraidia et al., doi:10.1038/s41598-026-42705-7, and Wynn, Satija and Hadfield,
  arXiv:2509.05396, report settings in which debate degrades accuracy.
- `[cited]` Zhou shows that a convincing judge receipt can diverge sharply from hidden correctness;
  verification display can manufacture trust rather than deserve it.
- `[asserted]` Source badges and test counts may become placebic explanations. They can make an answer
  look rigorous while hiding an irrelevant source, weak oracle, shifted beta or losing alternative
  that was never viable.
- `[asserted]` A frontier Owner with retrieval, execution, browser access and the whole budget may
  preserve more relevant context, answer faster and be preferred to any partitioned organisation.

The strongest case is therefore that the honest product is one excellent model with good tools and
no committee. This decision concedes that case by making one Owner the default, hiding organisation,
giving identity and agreement zero evidence credit, and requiring the blind same-budget `EXP-128`
comparison before activation or a visible-superiority claim. [asserted]

## Consequences

**Positive** — the user receives one useful response whose decision-relevant checks can be inspected
without reading process theatre; trivial work avoids squad cost; adverse and unresolved evidence
remain reachable. [asserted]

**Negative** — source binding, execution, alternatives and distinct-anchor acquisition add latency,
tokens and review burden; a compact receipt can still overstate a weak check; preference measurement
requires consented human ratings. [asserted]

**Neutral but load-bearing** — ADR-0067 retains composition, ADR-0081 retains consequence gating,
fusion/exposure remains an unresolved upstream dependency, `routing_orchestration_enabled` stays
false, the existing CLI surface is unchanged, and the principal's authority remains undelegable.
[measured] [asserted]

## What would overturn this

Once written unchanged to the experiment register, EXP-128 confirms activation only if the treatment
wins at least 123 of 220 prompt-level blind material-preference majorities, is non-inferior on
correctness within the frozen five-point margin, has no arm-B critical factual error, fabricated
citation or fabricated execution receipt, routes at least 50 of 55 simple prompts directly, and
remains inside its token and latency ceilings. [asserted]

At most 110 B material-preference majorities, a correctness loss beyond the margin, an arm-B
critical factual error, fabricated citation, fabricated execution receipt or a cost-ceiling breach
defeats the decision. Counts 111–122 or an incomplete run are inconclusive and also retain the
single-Owner default. If the checked answer is more correct but less liked, the visible-quality claim
loses; required checks remain only where consequence justifies them, and the presentation must be
redesigned and re-tested. [asserted]
