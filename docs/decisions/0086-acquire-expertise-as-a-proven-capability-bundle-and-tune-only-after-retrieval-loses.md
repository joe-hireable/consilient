## Context

The principal wants explicit and inferred targeted self-learning which can culminate in specialist
models and specialist squads. “Learning an expertise” currently conflates observation, retrieval,
capability persistence, evaluation, training and assignment. ADR-0074 makes the decisive boundary:
training begins only when learned model state persistently mutates. [measured] [asserted]

The inferred path carries a higher consent burden because the user did not request acquisition.
Repeated interactions alone are not evidence of a durable domain need, and an inferred training run
would spend compute and reuse private data on a judgement the user never made. ADR-0057 keeps the
trajectory private; ADR-0064 leaves hosted training unavailable until its joint budget/allowlist is
implemented. [measured] [asserted]

Current capability selection, instruction assembly, work items, claims, routing, budgets, dispatch
and trajectory records are reusable substrates, but the manifest, sealed host, authenticated owner
ingress and compliant specialist assignment do not exist. `consil doctor` reports both gates failed
and routing disabled on 22 August 2026. [measured]

This is reversible architecture. A bad trigger can be disabled, a bundle can be retired from
selection, and a checkpoint can be rolled back while immutable source and adverse-outcome records
remain. Deployment beyond this repository is a separate Gate B and typed-effect decision. [asserted]

## Decision

Consilient will represent expertise as a private, immutable, versioned **capability bundle** whose
active head may be assigned directly to one supervised work item. Retrieved sources, a skill, tool
configuration, worked examples, provenance, licence/consent, validity rules, sealed evaluation and
outcome history are the ordinary bundle. A tuned checkpoint is an optional separately qualified
capability reference, not the expertise's default or source of truth. Automatic library selection
remains inert pending EXP-101. [asserted]

One authenticated explicit “learn X” imperative authorises bounded local acquisition from licensed
public sources and current-task material in quarantine without another confirmation, after the
independent instrument is frozen. Authentication requires ADR-0075/0076/0078's future owner-only
ingress, not caller-supplied actor metadata; it is unavailable today. The request does not authorise
importing prior private trajectory content into the bundle, trajectory training, parameter mutation,
spend, activation, publication or actuation. [measured] [asserted]

An inferred trigger may only propose. Over a rolling 90-day window it requires at least six
completed applicable work items spanning 21 days/four calendar days, three distinct postcondition
signatures, and either two different-class adverse signals or 180 measured repeated-acquisition
minutes across three work items. A conservative half-recurrence estimate must remain positive, and
a rejected/expired proposal suppresses recurrence for 90 days absent a new independent critical
failure. Before new study or compute, acceptance must arrive as a fresh, single-use first-party
receipt bound to the proposal, scope, privacy boundary, resource ceiling, expiry and nonce; a child
cannot mint, widen or replay it. [asserted]

Before acquisition, an instrument author independent of the acquisition, candidate, trainer and
promoter freezes and seals the task bank, truth contracts, comparator, budgets, overlap rule,
critical errors, missing-data policy and stopping rule. Each registered candidate lineage receives a
disjoint one-use qualification batch, retired once its verdict affects activation. A user's corpus is
split first; held-out items, answers and semantic siblings never enter retrieval, examples or
training. No competent independent instrument means no deployment. [asserted]

The first outcome comparison is always the unchanged eligible generalist versus the same generalist
plus the bundle. EXP-126 owns that comparison and can qualify only direct, supervised, explicitly
selected assignment. A loss/tie, bundle-caused critical error or registered harm result discards the
specialist assignment while preserving its history; an inconclusive result remains quarantined.
Failed expertise is a normal outcome. EXP-101 separately owns automatic library selection.
[asserted]

Only a stable behavioural/representation/latency deficit remaining after correct retrieval, exact
rights and consent, positive reuse economics, local-first feasibility and dynamic-fact exclusion may
reach ADR-0085. That lifecycle owns base choice, fitting, checkpoint qualification, model-update
routing, cost comparison and rollback. For expertise assignment, its evaluation or a successor must
also show incremental value over the same bundle without parameter mutation. EXP-111 currently
compares an adapter with its unmodified base and the incumbent, so it does not by itself isolate that
delta. Tuned expertise remains unavailable until ADR-0085 owns a separately pre-registered,
fresh-bank, same-base-plus-same-bundle comparison. [measured] [asserted]

Assignment consumes ADR-0067 and ADR-0082's work-item RACI: one Owner is accountable; a specialist
is a task-scoped responsible role; consulted roles exist only for a structurally different anchor;
informed roles gain no rights. A consulted role may make a candidate ineligible only when its sealed
observation proves a necessary condition frozen before acquisition failed; it cannot decide, vote or
rewrite the condition. One bundle shared across a squad is one anchor, so additional same-bundle
specialists are echo and are removed. ADR-0081's high-consequence pair must come from a separately
acquired execution, browser, primary-source or novel-corpus/public-API anchor; a different model
family alone has zero structural credit. Every squad emits one Owner candidate. [asserted]

Wrong or stale expertise is never rewritten. Source/tool/model change, expiry, two attributable
human rejections in 20 applicable assignments, or a stronger generalist triggers re-evaluation on a
fresh pre-allocated qualification or drift-sentinel batch; the admitting batch is never replayed.
One critical bundle-caused error, privacy/authority breach or invalid licence/consent immediately
quarantines. New versions supersede old ones only after a fresh one-use qualification; a caught-up
comparator, unreachable break-even or vanished domain retires the active head while history stays
addressable. [asserted]

The full state, handoff and experiment contract is specified in
`../superpowers/specs/2026-08-22-expertise-acquisition.md`. [asserted]

## Evidence

- `[measured]` ADR-0074 and the active source tree expose reusable capability, instruction,
  work-item, coordination, recall, dispatch, routing, budget and event paths, while explicitly
  recording that the manifest/activation contract is not implemented.
- `[measured]` `consil doctor` reported Gate A FAIL, Gate B FAIL and routing disabled on 22 August
  2026; no document here activates assignment or device control.
- `[measured]` The repository's eight-system self-extension audit found persistent skills and
  harnesses, but no system in that bounded sample reported pre-persistence denominator-based false
  acceptance against independent truth.
- `[cited]` Zhang et al. (2026), *Ratchet: How Reliable Must an LLM Judge Be to Retire a Skill?*,
  arXiv:2605.22148v3, show bounded skill retirement and the failure mode of library drift.
- `[cited]` Wang et al. (2026), *Rethinking the Evaluation of Harness Evolution for Agents*,
  arXiv:2607.12227v1, found no significant held-out gain for its evolved harness under the reported
  matched-budget disjoint split; one benchmark and two runs do not establish a universal negative.
- `[algebra]` The paired treatment-minus-control success difference in EXP-126 is bounded by
  `[-1, +1]`; the bundle can repair every baseline failure or poison every baseline success.
- `[asserted]` A proposal-only inferred trigger, bundle-first acquisition, sealed comparison and
  append-only retirement is the smallest design which can satisfy the request without treating
  retrieval as training or inferred utility as consent.

## Evidence against

The strongest case is that targeted expertise acquisition is not worth building. Frontier general
models may improve faster than a specialist can be collected and maintained, making every bundle a
short-lived cache with a costly evaluation ceremony. A bundle built from one user's interactions may
overfit their habits and recurring errors rather than the domain, then amplify those habits under an
“expert” label. [asserted]

Retrieval plus a good skill may already capture nearly all useful value at a fraction of tuning's
data, compute, privacy, rollback and decay burden. Ratchet demonstrates both the promise and the
failure mode of skill libraries; Wang et al.'s matched-budget result warns that an improvement loop
can add cost without held-out benefit. Neither supports automatic model training as the default.
[cited] [asserted]

The decision concedes most of this objection. It does not build automatic tuning, does not activate
inferred acquisition and does not give “expert” status for study alone. It preserves only the cheap,
reversible bundle candidate and makes the unchanged generalist the incumbent it must beat. If
EXP-126 fails, specialist assignment is retired for that expertise; explicit retrieval and manual
skill use remain without the expertise claim. Automatic selection remains governed by EXP-101
whatever this experiment reports. [asserted]

Known weaknesses remain material: trigger thresholds and decay counts are judgement values; the
sealed host/authenticated ingress do not exist; one frozen expertise cannot establish transfer; a
human/domain verdict may be unavailable; source overlap can evade a semantic-sibling audit; and the
ADR-0085/EXP-111 design which landed during drafting does not isolate tuned-over-bundle value.
[measured] [asserted]

## Consequences

**Positive** — expertise has inspectable sources, a narrow contract, an honest training boundary, a
generalist comparator, normal discard, task-scoped assignment and reversible retirement. Inferred
learning cannot silently spend the user's compute or repurpose private interaction data. [asserted]

**Negative** — independent held-out authoring, overlap checks and requalification are expensive;
conservative triggers miss useful niches; most candidate bundles may never activate; and every
active bundle adds freshness and outcome-accounting work. [asserted]

**Neutral but load-bearing** — explicit acquisition is not training consent; a qualified model is
not an independent evidence anchor; one specialist assignment is not a standing organisation;
manual/supervised retrieval remains available if specialist assignment fails; every gate and the
principal's reserved authority remain unchanged. [asserted]

## What would overturn this

- EXP-126 kills assignment for its frozen expertise under its registered loss, harm or critical-error
  rule; a confirmation authorises only direct, supervised, explicitly selected assignment of that
  frozen bundle after prerequisite controls exist. [asserted]
- A successor to EXP-111, or a revision fixed before EXP-111 outcomes, showing that a tuned checkpoint
  reliably adds material outcome/cost value over bundle-only would overturn the “rare exception”
  prior for its measured strata, not the retrieval/training boundary. [asserted]
- A blinded 90-day proposal audit in which more than half of eligible inferred proposals are rejected
  as irrelevant would retire or tighten the inferred threshold before another proposal cycle.
  [asserted]
- A current generalist matching or beating an active specialist under the frozen task/cost contract
  retires that specialist; a successor can be reconsidered only as a new immutable version.
  [asserted]
