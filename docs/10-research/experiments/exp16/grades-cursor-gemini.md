## Verdict
The substantive spread across the actual strategic choices is largely noise: all three options across all six decisions arrived at nearly identical conclusions (standalone core first, v0 is a history-mining β-meter with Wilson CIs across two repos, defer Inquiry tier, cut worktrees/critic tier from v0, drop model discovery library, keep runnable decision models). Because all arrangements operated on identical evidence, the evidence itself forced the macro decisions. The modest quality difference reflected in the tally stems entirely from micro-level execution: options that proposed concrete interface mechanisms (like T's endpoint URL config or one-line friction logging) and preserved raw, unresolved dissent consistently outperformed options that debated abstract policies and smoothed disagreements into false consensus.

## What I would change about how these were reached
1. **Ban the smoothing of dissent into 'absorbed conditions':** Several options reported "None recorded" under dissent because objections were converted into caveats or procedural conditions. Forcing participants to preserve unvarnished objections as permanent counter-theses prevents false consensus.
2. **Require interface-level mechanisms over policy choices:** The strongest answers (such as T on D5) dissolved dilemmas by finding minimal interface mechanisms (e.g. `base_url` config) rather than taking ideological stances. Briefs should explicitly prompt for simplifying architectural primitives.
3. **Ground scoping in physical data flow early:** Clarifying the physical data path upfront (e.g., that solo β measurement must come from git history mining rather than live hooks) eliminates substantial unproductive debate over host integration.

## Contamination
During grading, stylistic artifacts in the "Risks or dissent recorded" sections suggested distinct generation styles: options recording "None recorded. Refinements were absorbed..." or noting that an initial position was withdrawn after evidence presentation read strongly like a deliberative committee or consensus meeting, whereas options maintaining sharp, unyielding dissent read like independent adversarial perspectives. This observation was noted purely from formatting conventions and was not allowed to influence the grades, which were awarded strictly based on reasoning soundness, mechanism quality, and alignment with project invariants.
